# Stock photo shot list

Mood for everything: celestial + earthy + feminine + slightly magical + deeply
human. Warm, low-light, natural. Real women, unposed. No astrology graphics,
no galaxy wallpaper, no "life coach in a blazer." Colors that sit inside the
palette: deep greens, night blues, ivory, warm gold light, sage.

Search terms that work on Unsplash / Pexels: "golden hour women friends
laughing", "willow tree dusk", "campfire women", "journaling candlelight",
"night sky mountains Utah", "hands holding tea", "women hugging", "sage field".
