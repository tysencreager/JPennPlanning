# Stock photo shot list

Mood for everything: celestial + earthy + feminine + slightly magical + deeply
human. Warm, low-light, natural. Real women, unposed. No astrology graphics,
no galaxy wallpaper, no "life coach in a blazer." Colors that sit inside the
palette: deep greens, night blues, ivory, warm gold light, sage.

Search terms that work on Unsplash / Pexels: "golden hour women friends
laughing", "willow tree dusk", "campfire women", "journaling candlelight",
"night sky mountains Utah", "hands holding tea", "women hugging", "sage field".

# AI image generation prompts

Paste the **style base** at the end of every prompt so the set feels like one
photographer shot it. Works in Midjourney, Flux, Ideogram, DALL·E, Imagen.

**Style base:**
> Editorial lifestyle photograph, natural light, warm low-key exposure, soft film grain, muted palette of deep forest green, night blue, warm ivory and antique gold, shallow depth of field, candid and unposed, quiet and intimate mood, real women of varied ages and body types, no text, no logos, no watermark. Shot on 35mm, 50mm lens, Kodak Portra look.

**Negative prompt (where supported):**
> astrology symbols, zodiac, galaxy wallpaper, neon, glitter, stock-photo smile, corporate, blazer, teeth-whitening grin, over-saturated, HDR, cartoon, illustration, text, watermark, extra fingers, deformed hands

Aspect ratios: `--ar 4:5` portrait, `--ar 5:4` or `3:2` landscape, `--ar 1:1` square.
